Economic History of the People's Serbian Republic

People's Commercial Bank

The People's Commercial Bank was an economic and organizational institution established in Town Debug in early 2021.

Its origins can be traced to Comrade Željko Petrovic's encounter with the people operating the People's Oil Rig.

The workers and organizers of the People's Oil Rig followed the Juche ideal and had developed their own system of organization and faction management. After meeting them, Comrade Petrovic was convinced to assist with the management and development of their faction.

From this early cooperation, the People's Commercial Bank developed into an active organization with economic, administrative and faction-management functions.

The organization remained active throughout much of the following years and became associated with the economic history of Town Debug and the surrounding factions.

People's Oil Rig

The People's Oil Rig played an important role in the origins of the People's Commercial Bank.

Its members followed the Juche ideal and maintained a distinct factional organization in Town Debug.

The meeting between the People's Oil Rig community and Comrade Željko Petrovic in early 2021 became a significant moment in the subsequent development of the organization's economic structure.

Petrovic's assistance with faction management helped establish the organizational foundations from which the People's Commercial Bank would operate.

The Closure of the People's Commercial Bank

The People's Commercial Bank remained active from approximately 2021 until late 2025.

In late 2025, however, the organization was forced to shut down following another period of corrupt moderation.

Following the shutdown, the organization never returned to its previous level of activity. Although its legacy remains part of the economic history of the republic, the organization has not been fully active since its closure.

Economic Timeline

Early 2021 — Comrade Željko Petrovic encounters the people of the People's Oil Rig in Town Debug and begins assisting with faction management.

2021 — The People's Commercial Bank becomes active and begins establishing itself as an organizational and economic institution.

2021–2025 — The organization remains active and participates in the economic and factional affairs of the community.

Late 2025 — The People's Commercial Bank is shut down following another episode of corrupt moderation.

2026–Present — The organization has not returned to full activity.
